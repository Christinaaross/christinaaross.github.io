About Me

I am a researcher and computer scientist interested in building thoughtful, human-centered systems that combine rigorous technical foundations with clear, real-world impact. My work sits at the intersection of systems, data, and intelligent design, with a focus on developing tools and methods that help people understand, use, and benefit from technology in meaningful ways.
